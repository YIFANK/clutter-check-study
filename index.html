MIT 9.66 class project · about 6 minutes

What needs checking before we act?

You will see a table with six objects on it and decide whether a new disk fits at a marked spot. The objects' positions are only roughly known, and you can check up to two of them before you answer.

Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time. The study records which objects you check, your answers and their timing. It does not ask for your name or any other personal details. A mouse or trackpad on a laptop works best.
